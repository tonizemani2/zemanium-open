    Model boundary, parameter warnings and fair comparison

      24 cases separate temperature, flow, diameter and productivity index. Geometry is assumed: 1 km³, 20 fractures, 900 m height.       Calculation boundary and primary sources

      No new physics was generated by this cost sweep. The retained GEOPHIRES-X 3.18.3 trajectories assume a reservoir geometry and 90% utilization; no firm-capacity guarantee follows. Export deducts pumping and 5% of gross auxiliaries. Annual costs: 2% of installed capital, source make-up water and $8/exported MWh. Explicit events: $5m in year 12 and $2m in year 25. All capital is at time zero; construction interest is not modeled. Installed block, subsurface and other scope are unquoted allowances in constant real USD, not scope-complete estimates.

      The design preset requires 294 kg/s, 12-inch internal bore and long-lived contact in an assumed 1 km³ reservoir; it does not establish these in a real field. The 240 °C ORC extrapolations and importing/finite-inventory failures receive no price in this tab.
