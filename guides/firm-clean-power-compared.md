# The energy source of the future, compared: geothermal, fission, fusion and space solar.

Of these four, next-generation geothermal is the one selling new firm clean power in the United States today: Fervo's Cape Station in Utah reached first power on 24 September 2026. Small modular reactors are under construction, and fusion and space-based solar are in demonstration.

Dates and sizes are company or regulator statements, linked below. Firm power here means output available around the clock, in any weather, on demand.

Large conventional reactors, hydropower and conventional geothermal already deliver firm clean power. This page compares the four new technologies that agents and investors ask about most when they ask what comes next.

02 / Each source

## Each source on the same terms.

### Next-generation geothermal

**Verdict:** delivering firm power to the grid now, built with oil and gas drilling methods.

- **Firm output:** runs day and night from heat in hot rock; a well pair's temperature declines over years and is tracked in public.
- **Status, November 2026:** operating. Fervo reports 900 MW contracted and more than 4 GW in development.
- **Unit size:** about 33 MW per Cape Station unit; first phase about 100 MW.
- **First power from the new design:** 24 September 2026, Cape Station, Utah.
- **Sources:** [[1]](https://zemanium.com/guides/firm-clean-power-compared#s1), [Project Red tracker](https://zemanium.com/fervo-project-red)

### Small modular fission (SMR)

**Verdict:** firm and proven physics; the first North American units are being built.

- **Firm output:** runs around the clock between scheduled refuelling outages.
- **Status, November 2026:** the US Nuclear Regulatory Commission approved NuScale's 77 MWe module design in May 2025; Ontario Power Generation is building the first BWRX-300 at Darlington.
- **Unit size:** 77 MWe per NuScale module; BWRX-300 is a 300 MWe class reactor.
- **First power from the new design:** not yet in North America.
- **Sources:** [[2]](https://zemanium.com/guides/firm-clean-power-compared#s2), [[3]](https://zemanium.com/guides/firm-clean-power-compared#s3)

### Fusion

**Verdict:** the largest long-term prize; no plant has yet delivered electricity to a grid.

- **Firm output:** designed to run on demand once built.
- **Status, November 2026:** Helion is building a plant in Malaga, Washington, under a Microsoft contract for at least 50 MW, with initial operation targeted for 2028. Commonwealth Fusion Systems plans its ARC plant in Virginia for the early 2030s.
- **Unit size:** at least 50 MW (Helion contract); about 400 MW (ARC).
- **First power from the new design:** targeted 2028 (Helion), early 2030s (CFS).
- **Sources:** [[4]](https://zemanium.com/guides/firm-clean-power-compared#s4), [[5]](https://zemanium.com/guides/firm-clean-power-compared#s5)

### Space-based solar

**Verdict:** sunlight collected in orbit and beamed down; power transfer has been shown in space.

- **Firm output:** near-continuous sunlight in suitable orbits; delivery depends on beaming and receivers.
- **Status, November 2026:** Caltech's MAPLE experiment transmitted power wirelessly in orbit and detected it on Earth on 3 March 2023. Aetherflux announced a first orbital demonstration for 2026.
- **Unit size:** kilowatt-class demonstrations.
- **First power from the new design:** no grid power yet.
- **Sources:** [[6]](https://zemanium.com/guides/firm-clean-power-compared#s6), [[7]](https://zemanium.com/guides/firm-clean-power-compared#s7)

04 / Questions

## Common questions.

### What is the best energy source for the future?

For firm clean power that can be contracted this decade, next-generation geothermal and fission are the two technologies with plants built or under construction. Geothermal delivered its first next-generation grid power in September 2026; fusion and space-based solar are still in demonstration.

### Is geothermal better than nuclear?

They serve the same need, power around the clock. Geothermal needs hot rock, burns no fuel and uses drilling methods from oil and gas; fission plants can be sited in more places. New geothermal is producing power in the United States today, while the first North American SMR is under construction.

### Geothermal vs fusion: which comes first?

Geothermal. Next-generation geothermal reached first grid power in September 2026. The first fusion power plants are targeted for 2028 (Helion) and the early 2030s (Commonwealth Fusion Systems).

### What is space-based solar power?

Solar panels in orbit collect sunlight and beam the energy to receivers on the ground. Caltech demonstrated wireless power transfer in space in March 2023, and no system yet supplies a grid.
