# Geothermal companies in 2026: who is building what.

Most geothermal power today comes from long-established operators such as Ormat in the United States, Pertamina Geothermal Energy in Indonesia and KenGen in Kenya. A newer group is adding enhanced, closed-loop and superhot geothermal: Fervo Energy's Cape Station in Utah sent its first power on 24 September 2026, Eavor's closed-loop plant in Germany has delivered power since December 2025, and Sage Geosystems, XGS Energy, Zanskar, Quaise Energy and Mazama Energy have plants under way.

Milestones are the companies' own statements or press reports of them, dated and linked below. Capacity figures for listed producers are company statements. Independent summary; not investment advice. Zemanium holds no position in any security.

03 / Utilities and suppliers

## Utilities, operators and oilfield suppliers.

- Large utilities such as Enel and Berkshire Hathaway Energy own geothermal plants as a small part of wider fleets. In Nevada's regulator record Enel is the operator of record at Salt Wells and Stillwater.
- Geysers Power Company, a subsidiary of Calpine, runs The Geysers in California, which NLR calls the most prolific geothermal field in the United States.
- Oilfield service companies sell into geothermal wells: Baker Hughes is providing engineering for XGS Energy's New Mexico project, and SLB interpreted Utah FORGE's production logs for the Department of Energy.

04 / Buyers

## Who is buying next-generation geothermal power?

Data center owners and utilities. Meta has agreements for 150 MW each with Sage Geosystems and XGS Energy. Fervo built its Project Red pilot in partnership with Google, according to Canary Media. NV Energy signed a 20-year agreement with Zanskar for 100 MW by 2030. Public geothermal power contracts signed between November 2019 and June 2025 priced at $67.50 to $99 per megawatt-hour, according to the National Laboratory of the Rockies.

06 / Questions

## Common questions.

### What are the biggest geothermal companies?

By installed capacity, Pertamina Geothermal Energy reports 1,932 MW in Indonesia including partner-operated plants, and Ormat Technologies a 1,853 MW portfolio that includes solar and storage. KenGen runs 754 MW of geothermal in Kenya.

### What companies are doing enhanced geothermal?

Fervo Energy, which reached first power at Cape Station in Utah on 24 September 2026, Sage Geosystems, with a 3 MW pilot in Texas, and Mazama Energy, drilling superhot wells in Oregon. The Department of Energy selected Fervo, Zanskar, AlterG Resources, Quaise Energy and the University of Utah for field-scale enhanced geothermal tests in September 2026.

### Is Fervo Energy a public company?

Yes. Fervo Energy trades on Nasdaq under the ticker FRVO after pricing its initial public offering at $27 a share on 12 May 2026.

### What is closed-loop geothermal?

A system that circulates fluid through sealed wells in hot rock and takes heat by conduction, with no fracturing and no water drawn from the rock. Eavor's plant at Geretsried, Germany, connects 12 lateral wells into six pairs and has sent power to the grid since December 2025, by the company's account.

### Which geothermal startups have raised the most money?

Among the developers on this page, Quaise Energy closed a $180m Series B in August 2026, Mazama Energy raised $135m in September 2026, and Zanskar raised a $115m Series C in January 2026, bringing its equity to $180m. Fervo Energy raised public equity in its May 2026 listing.
