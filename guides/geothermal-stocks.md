# Geothermal stocks in 2026: the listed producers and what their plants deliver.

The two US-listed companies that run mainly on geothermal power are Ormat Technologies (NYSE: ORA), the largest, and Fervo Energy (Nasdaq: FRVO), listed in May 2026. Six more listed companies in Indonesia, Kenya, New Zealand and Canada own large geothermal fleets. Below is each one, its capacity from its own reports, and the public plant record behind the numbers.

Capacity figures are company statements. Nevada output figures are measured and operator-reported to the state regulator. Independent analysis from public records; not investment advice. Zemanium holds no position in any security.

02 / Fervo

## Fervo Energy (FRVO): what the public record shows.

Fervo priced its initial public offering at $27 a share on 12 May 2026 and sold 70 million shares; trading on Nasdaq began on 13 May. Its first commercial plant, Cape Station in Utah, reached first power on 24 September 2026, with a first phase of about 100 MW planned, by the company's account.

Fervo's pilot well pair, Project Red in Nevada, is the only enhanced geothermal system with a public monthly production record. Its wellhead temperature read 347 °F at its 2024 plateau and 316 °F in July 2026. We track it every month against a forecast registered on 19 November 2026.

03 / Record

## What Nevada's geothermal plants produced, year by year.

Nevada publishes monthly output for every geothermal plant in the state. Statewide output was 5,044 GWh in 2019, peaked at 5,391 GWh in 2023 and was 5,031 GWh in 2025. The McGinness Hills complex, whose operator of record is Ormat Nevada and its ORNI subsidiaries, is the largest single site in the record.

04 / Method

## How to read a geothermal producer.

- **Capacity factor.** Energy produced in a year divided by capacity times 8,760 hours. Geothermal plants run day and night, so a falling figure usually means the wells deliver less heat.
- **Reservoir temperature trend.** A well that cools needs makeup drilling to hold output. The regulator records in Nevada show it field by field.
- **Cost per megawatt-hour.** Our [cost guide](https://zemanium.com/guides/geothermal-electricity-cost) shows which inputs move it, and the [power-cost model](https://zemanium.com/confidence/) prices a plant under your assumptions.

05 / Questions

## Common questions.

### Is Fervo Energy publicly traded?

Yes. Fervo Energy trades on Nasdaq under the ticker FRVO. It priced its initial public offering at $27 a share on 12 May 2026 and began trading on 13 May 2026.

### What is the largest geothermal stock?

By capacity, Pertamina Geothermal Energy (IDX: PGEO) reports 1,932 MW installed including partner-operated plants. Among US-listed companies, Ormat Technologies (NYSE: ORA) is the largest, with a 1,853 MW portfolio that also includes solar and storage.

### Are there geothermal stocks outside the United States?

Yes. Pertamina Geothermal Energy and Barito Renewables trade in Jakarta, KenGen in Nairobi, Mercury NZ and Contact Energy in New Zealand, and Polaris Renewable Energy in Toronto.

### Where can I check a geothermal plant's actual output?

In Nevada, the Division of Minerals publishes monthly output for every plant and monthly temperature for every well. Our Nevada plant file and the Project Red tracker take their numbers from that record. Across the United States, the EIA publishes monthly generation by plant.
