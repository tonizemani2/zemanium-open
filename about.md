# About Zemanium.

Zemanium is a geothermal invention lab. We design the instruments, well hardware and models that hot wells need, test them in simulation against rules we fix beforehand, and publish what comes out.

02 / Mission

## Make Earth's heat the world's power.

Our mission is to make geothermal the world's dominant energy source. Heat under our feet is available day and night in every country, and the obstacle is cost. We work on the equipment that decides that cost: wells that survive, flow that reaches the hot rock, instruments that keep working, and plants that convert more of the heat.

Our development target is firm geothermal power at $30 to $45 per megawatt-hour. The [cost guide](https://zemanium.com/guides/geothermal-electricity-cost) shows what a plant has to cost to get there.

03 / Method

## How we work.

1. Every invention goes through a simulated test campaign before any metal is cut. We write down the mechanism, the equations, the pass and fail rules and the conditions that would prove us wrong, and then we run it.
2. The rules that judge a simulation are the rules that will judge the bench test.
3. Every number carries a label: measured, vendor-stated, SIMULATED, assumption. A simulated result is never called a qualification.
4. When a design fails its test we publish the failure and say what it changes.
